Research & AI

Responsible analysis for questions that affect people.

My graduate direction sits at the intersection of computer science, artificial intelligence, biostatistics, data quality, accessibility, and public health.

Research direction

Human-centered AI requires more than model performance.

I am interested in systems that are technically useful, transparent about uncertainty, attentive to data quality, and designed around the people affected by their outputs. My media and communication background adds a practical emphasis on accessibility and translating technical findings clearly.

Current themes

  • Responsible and human-centered AI
  • Health disparities and reproductive health
  • Data quality, missingness, and model limitations
  • Human review in automated workflows
  • Accessible metadata and information retrieval
  • Clear communication of statistical findings
